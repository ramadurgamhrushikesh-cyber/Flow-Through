How to Play

1

Start at the glowing junction

Every level has a pulsing split-cell. Drag from it to begin drawing your pipe.

2

Fill every cell

Your pipe must pass through every single cell in the grid — no gaps allowed.

3

Junctions switch your color

Each split junction changes your color. Inner shape = incoming color. Outer shape = outgoing color. The shape changes each season!

4

Visit junctions in chain order

Follow the color chain shown at the top. Visit each junction in order — no skipping allowed.

5

Close the loop to win

After filling the grid and visiting all junctions, drag back into the first junction to complete the circuit!

6

Each season has a new junction shape

Season 1 uses rounded squares. Season 2 uses circles. Season 3 triangles. Season 4 hexagons. Season 5 diamonds. Same grid, new visual language.
