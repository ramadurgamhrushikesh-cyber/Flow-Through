How to Play

1

Start at the glowing junction

Every level has a pulsing split-cell. Drag from it to begin drawing your pipe.

2

Fill every cell

Your pipe must pass through every single cell in the grid — no gaps allowed.

3

Junctions switch colors

Each split junction changes your color. The inner shape is the incoming color, the outer shape is the outgoing color.

4

Visit all junctions in order

Follow the color chain shown at the top. Each color must enter its junction — no skipping.

5

Seasons change the grid

Season 1 uses square grids. Future seasons use circles, triangles, hexagons and diamonds — each with unique rules.
